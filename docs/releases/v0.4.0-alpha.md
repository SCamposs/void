## Stacker

- A larger responsive playfield with persistent hold and five-piece preview rails, a prominent clock and clear ready, paused and finished states.
- Seven distinct neon tints, cold edge highlights, shape-colored glow and a more readable outlined ghost. Existing palette and skin selections remain available.
- Short hard-drop trails, placement highlights and clear effects that follow the affected cells. Reduced motion removes particles, flashes, trails and shake.
- The **VOID glacial** sound pack adapts 23 TETR.IO recordings with shorter tails, filtering, restrained levels and a shared mix. Original audio remains credited to OSK; provenance is retained with the assets.
- Distinct cues for movement, rotation, hold, drop, placement, ordinary clears, quads, spins, back-to-back quads, combos, all clears and run completion.
- Quick mute in the header, immediate master-volume control and separate sound previews in settings. Existing procedural sound packs remain available as alternatives and loading fallbacks.

## Fixes

- Stable rotation pivots using fixed piece frames instead of cropped rectangles.
- DAS/ARR repeat timing carries time across frames and supports multiple repeats when needed.
- T-spins are classified before line removal, and downward travel preserves rotation intent.
- Hold and hard drop give the next piece a fresh gravity interval.
- Sprint stops accepting placement input immediately upon reaching 40 lines.
- Controls respond on GO; handling presets preserve the training seed and opener.
- Bundled sound loading is permitted under the packaged app security policy.
- Opening settings pauses an Endless run; settings remain unavailable during an active sprint. Fast placements retain an accurate piece count.
- Blocked movement and unavailable hold no longer play confirmation sounds. DAS cut no longer reapplies every frame after a placement.

## Verification and limitations

Automated validation covers engine regressions, browser gameplay and audio, responsive layouts, reduced motion, frontend checks and native packaging. The release pipeline independently validates, signs and verifies the Windows NSIS and Linux DEB/AppImage packages.

- TETR.IO is a reference for this refinement. Exact gameplay or timing equivalence is not claimed.
- Windows installers remain unsigned by Authenticode; updater signatures are separate. Download from this repository and verify the adjacent checksum.
- Real upgrade/restart, native DPI and final installer acceptance remain tracked in [manual validation #2](https://github.com/SCamposs/void/issues/2).
