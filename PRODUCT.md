# Product

## Register

product

## Users

VOID is for a solo desktop user who wants compact local tools with a distinctive, low-distraction interface. The app is used in focused sessions, often full-window, where keyboard flow matters but the product should still feel like a real graphical desktop app.

## Product Purpose

VOID is a desktop-first tool shell that hosts focused modules such as typing practice, orbit visuals, and Stacker. Success means each module feels usable, local, polished, and intentionally shaped, not like a terminal prototype with a web skin.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, liminal, precise. VOID should feel like a modern desktop app viewed through old monitor texture: charcoal surfaces, ice-white text, restrained motion, and no cyberpunk neon.

## Anti-references

Avoid green terminal themes, colorful dashboard palettes, fake productivity SaaS polish, heavy rounded cards, loud arcade effects, and visual effects that distract from the active module.

## Design Principles

- Keep the retro monitor texture stable and subtle.
- Prioritize dense, readable desktop workflows over decorative screens.
- Make every module feel complete enough to use, even when it stays local-first.
- Use motion and sound as feedback, not spectacle.
- Preserve the VOID palette and sharp panel language across modules.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect reduced-motion preferences, avoid constant flicker, maintain readable contrast on dark surfaces, and keep controls operable by keyboard and pointer.
